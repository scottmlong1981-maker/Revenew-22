How To Get Started

A simple first conversation.

Getting started begins with Discovery — a focused conversation designed to determine whether a meaningful Revenew opportunity exists within your organization.

The First Step

Discovery determines whether a legitimate Revenew opportunity exists.

The goal of Discovery is not to sell. The goal is to determine whether a meaningful opportunity exists. A successful discovery process creates clarity for both parties before any engagement begins.

The prospect gains a better understanding of what may be possible. Revenew gains a better understanding of the organization's content, objectives, and opportunities. When both parties agree an opportunity exists, the next step is a proposal.

A Different Starting Point

You do not need to organize everything first.

Most clients do not begin with a perfectly organized repository. Content often exists across presentations, reports, videos, workshops, shared drives, cloud storage, email archives, and internal systems. This is normal.

The objective is not to organize everything before beginning. The objective is to identify what exists. Organization follows discovery, not the other way around.

What To Expect
  • Revenew performs most of the collection, organization, analysis, and synthesis.
  • You provide direction, context, and clarification.
  • Start with your most valuable content — a perfect inventory is not required.
  • Undocumented expertise can be surfaced through interviews and working sessions.
