The Problem

The content exists. The structure around it does not.

A podcast can hold dozens or hundreds of valuable conversations. Most of that value lives on platforms the show does not control.

On YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts, episodes become a feed. The newest one is visible. Everything else slips further down the list.

Guests disappear into the archive. Expertise becomes hard to find. The people who could matter most to the show have no single place to understand what it is and why it matters: listeners, clients, sponsors, partners, and future guests.

The problem is not a lack of content. It is the missing infrastructure around it.

Where Most Podcasts Stand
  • No dedicated website
  • A podcast page buried inside another site
  • Reliance on YouTube, Spotify, or Apple alone
  • An archive too large to browse
  • Guests and expertise that are not being put to use
  • A show disconnected from the business or mission behind it
Possibilities

More than a list of episodes.

A podcast website can take many forms. The structure depends on the show: its content, its audience, and what the podcast is meant to accomplish.

Episode Library
Every episode organized by theme, topic, or season, not by date alone.
Guest Directory
Each guest presented with their expertise, so past conversations keep working for both of you.
Video Hub
Full episodes from YouTube, presented on the show’s own site.
Listening Hub
Direct paths to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and every platform where the show is available.
Expertise Archive
Searchable topics that turn years of conversations into a reference people return to.
Resource Center
Supporting material and next steps related to the show’s subject.
Lead Generation Asset
Clear calls to action that connect listeners to the host’s business, services, or mission.
Sponsor & Partnership Destination
One place for sponsors and partners to understand the show and start a conversation.
Guest Recruitment Tool
An application that brings guests to the show rather than the other way around.

Not every podcast needs every feature. The structure is decided during Discovery, based on the show’s goals and the content it already has. Every site works on phones as well as desktops, carries the podcast’s own branding, and is built so new episodes can be added as the show continues.
